Simple 4-Step Workflow

How the Service Works

Nick makes the process simple, efficient, and reliable. Here's what to expect.

  1. I

    Submit Your Documents

    Complete the secure online intake form and upload your legal documents. Add any important details or special instructions so service can be completed efficiently.

  2. II

    Review & Confirm

    Nick reviews your documents and contacts you promptly to confirm details, pricing, and service timelines.

  3. III

    Service Attempts Begin

    Once payment is received by Zelle or credit/debit card, attempts begin promptly and are planned around the information you provided. Rush and same-day options are available when needed.

  4. IV

    Updates & Proof of Service

    Get consistent updates by text message, email, and/or phone, including real-time updates on service attempts. Once service is complete, proof of service is provided promptly.

Please note: each defendant is considered a separate service, and attempts are made at one address per service request.

Process serving FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about service areas, attempts, payment, and proof of service.

What types of legal documents can you serve?
Nick serves summons and complaints, subpoenas (civil, criminal, and federal), writs of garnishment, small claims documents, eviction notices, divorce papers, child custody and support orders, restraining orders, orders to show cause, and debt collection notices.
Which cities do you serve?
Long Beach, Carson, Compton, Lakewood, Paramount, Signal Hill, Bellflower, San Pedro, and Wilmington. Additional cities not listed may also be available on a case-by-case basis, so call or text Nick at (209) 922-6817 to ask.
How many service attempts are included?
Every tier includes 3 diligent attempts, made in the morning, evening, and night. Standard service spreads them over 10 days. Expedited and Same-Day Rush spread them over 5 days, with the first attempt within 48 hours or 24 hours of receiving your documents.
How and when do I pay?
After Nick reviews your documents and confirms the details, pricing, and timelines with you, payment is accepted by Zelle or credit/debit card. Service attempts begin promptly once payment is received.
How will I know where my service stands?
You'll get consistent updates by text message, email, and/or phone, including real-time updates on service attempts and status. Once service is completed, proof of service documentation is provided promptly, and immediately upon completion for Same-Day Rush.
Do you offer notary services?
Yes. Nick is a commissioned California Notary Public, so your legal documents can be notarized as well as delivered. Contact Nick with your notary request.
Are you registered and bonded in California?
Yes. All serves are performed by a Registered California Process Server bonded in accordance with California Business and Professions Code § 22350 et seq., affording your Proof of Service the statutory presumption of valid service under Evidence Code § 647.
