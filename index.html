California Process Serving FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about statutory compliance, attempts, and proof of service.

What types of legal documents can you serve?
We serve all California civil, family, probate, and small claims documents—including Summons & Complaints, Unlawful Detainers (evictions), 3/30/60-Day Notices, Civil & Deposition Subpoenas, Restraining Orders (TRO/DVRO), Wage Garnishments, and Orders for Appearance and Examination (ORAP).
What happens if the person is avoiding service or not home?
Under California Code of Civil Procedure § 415.20, once diligent attempts have been documented at different times of day (morning, afternoon, evening), many documents qualify for Substituted Service on a competent member of the household or person in charge at a workplace, followed by first-class mailing and a Declaration of Due Diligence.
How quickly will I receive my Proof of Service (POS-010)?
As soon as service is completed, you receive an instant email alert with the date, time, recipient description, and GPS coordinates. Your court-compliant California Judicial Council Proof of Service PDF is emailed within 24 hours (or same-day for Rush/Expedited orders).
Are you registered and bonded in California?
Yes. All serves are performed by Registered California Process Servers bonded in accordance with California Business and Professions Code § 22350 et seq., affording your Proof of Service the statutory presumption of valid service under Evidence Code § 647.
